Emergency civic complaints

In a life-threatening emergency, call 112 (India’s emergency number) immediately.

For urgent civic hazards – a fallen tree, a burst water main, an open manhole, a flooded road – register a complaint online with a photograph and mention “URGENT” in the description. Also call the ward office if a number is listed.
